# Zero: The Future of Email is Here

Email has been the backbone of digital communication for decades, yet most email clients haven't evolved much since the early 2000s. Enter **Zero** - an open-source, AI-powered email solution that's reimagining how we interact with our inbox.

## What Makes Zero Different?

In a world dominated by closed-source email providers that harvest your data, Zero stands out with its commitment to:

### 🔒 **Privacy First**
Your emails belong to you, not to big tech companies. Zero doesn't track, collect, or sell your data. With self-hosting capabilities, you maintain complete control over your communications.

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Intelligence**
Zero integrates cutting-edge AI agents and Large Language Models (LLMs) to enhance your email experience:
- **Smart Categorization**: Automatically organize your emails with intelligent labeling
- **Response Suggestions**: Get AI-powered reply suggestions that match your tone
- **Email Summarization**: Quickly understand long email threads with AI summaries
- **Priority Detection**: Let AI identify your most important emails

### 🏠 **Self-Hosting Freedom**
Unlike traditional email services, Zero gives you the power to:
- Host your own email infrastructure
- Connect multiple email providers (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) in one unified inbox
- Customize every aspect of your email experience
- Scale according to your needs

## The Technology Behind Zero

Zero is built with modern, reliable technologies that ensure performance and scalability:

```typescript
// Example: AI-powered email categorization
const categorizeEmail = async (email: Email) => {
  const aiResponse = await aiAgent.analyze({
    subject: email.subject,
    content: email.content,
    sender: email.from
  });
  
  return {
    category: aiResponse.category,
    priority: aiResponse.priority,
    suggestedLabels: aiResponse.labels
  };
};
```

### Tech Stack Highlights:
- **Frontend**: Next.js with React and TypeScript for a modern, responsive UI
- **Backend**: Node.js with Drizzle ORM for robust data management
- **Database**: PostgreSQL for reliable data storage
- **Authentication**: Better Auth with OAuth integration
- **Styling**: TailwindCSS with Shadcn UI components

1. **Transparency**: Every line of code is open for inspection
2. **Community-Driven Development**: Features built by users, for users
3. **Security**: No hidden backdoors or data collection mechanisms
4. **Extensibility**: Build custom features and integrations
5. **Long-term Sustainability**: Not dependent on a single company's business model

## Getting Started with Zero

Setting up Zero is straightforward:

```bash

# Install dependencies

```

Within minutes, you'll have a fully functional email client running locally, ready to connect to your existing email accounts.
